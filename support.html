FAQ

Prayer times look wrong? Make sure location access is allowed and pick the calculation method used in your country in Settings.

Compass inaccurate? Keep the phone away from metal and magnets, and move it in a figure-8 to calibrate.

No alerts? Go to iPhone Settings → Notifications → Salatuna and allow notifications.
